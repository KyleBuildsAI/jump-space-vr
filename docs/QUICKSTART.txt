JUMP SPACE VR - QUICK START
===========================

You need:
  - Jump Space (Steam), Windows 10/11
  - A PC VR headset (Quest via Steam Link / Virtual Desktop / Quest Link, Index, Vive, ...)

INSTALL (2 minutes)
  1. Close Jump Space.
  2. Double-click Install.bat. It finds the game, installs MelonLoader, the VR runtime files and the mod.
     (If .NET 6 is missing it is installed automatically; say yes if Windows asks.)
  3. Start your VR app first: SteamVR (Steam Link), Virtual Desktop, or the Meta Quest Link app.
  4. Launch Jump Space from Steam. The FIRST launch takes a few extra minutes (MelonLoader prepares the game).
     VR starts by itself once the game is loaded. No headset yet? It keeps retrying every 10 seconds.

CONTROLS
  Right controller ........ aim (point where you want to shoot)
  Right trigger ........... fire        Left trigger ...... aim down sights
  Left stick .............. move        Right stick ....... snap turn (smooth turn in the menu)
  Grips ................... bumpers     A / B / X / Y ..... face buttons
  Left grip at the gun's front (Weapon in hand on) ... hold the gun with both hands
  Left menu button ........ pause (hold 1 s to recenter your view)
  In menus ................ point at the screen and pull the trigger to click
  Gun pointed down ........ the game looks where you look: press X to use what you look at

  Insert (or hold right thumbstick click 1 s) ... VR settings menu

  GAMEPAD (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro): just pick it up, the mod switches to it.
  The game's normal pad controls work; hold View/Select (PlayStation: Share/Create) 1 s for the VR menu;
  click both sticks to recenter. Touch the VR controllers to switch back.
  F7 ... recenter     F8 ... VR on/off     F9 ... diagnostics     F10 ... save what the headset sees

CO-OP
  Beta: friends who also have the mod can see your hands move, and you see theirs. Everyone, including the host,
  turns on "Multiplayer hands (co-op)" in the VR menu (Weapon and hands).

PROBLEMS?
  - Choppy on a mid-range card (RTX 3060/4060/5060)? Insert menu > "Preset: slower graphics card",
    and set Steam Link / SteamVR to 72 or 90 Hz.
  - Reporting a problem: press F10 and F9 in game, then send MelonLoader\Latest.log (it includes a
    [session] summary of frame rate and controller input every 5 minutes).
  - Game stays flat: start SteamVR / Virtual Desktop / Quest Link and wait 10 seconds.
  - Wrong VR app picked: Insert menu > VR runtime (Auto / SteamVR / VirtualDesktop / MetaLink),
    then press F8 twice to restart VR.
  - Low frame rate: Insert menu > Render scale.
  - Gun sits wrong in your hand: Insert menu > Weapon offset X/Y/Z.
  - Remove the mod: double-click Uninstall.bat (or Steam > Verify integrity of game files).

Please play solo or with friends who know you're using a mod. Don't send crash reports from the modded game
to the developers.
